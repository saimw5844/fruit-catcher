🎮 How to play

Move the basket with your mouse, by dragging on touch screens, or with ← → / A D.

Build combos: every catch in a row raises your multiplier — ×2, ×3 and more. A miss or a bomb resets it.

Level up every 150 points: fruit falls faster and more often. You have 3 lives. Press P or Esc to pause.
